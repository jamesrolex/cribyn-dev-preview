Services

Four levels of enquiry. Be clear which one you are buying.

Most of what is sold as due diligence is a database search with a cover sheet. That is sometimes all a matter needs, and we will say so. But the word covers four quite different things, each including the one before it, and the difference between them is where the risk sits.

Level one
Screening
A compliance answer: is this name on a list. Fast, cheap, and only as good as the lists.
Sanctions and PEP databases
Adverse media aggregators
Corporate registry extracts
Watchlist and enforcement lists
Buy this when
A regulator requires it, or the counterparty is low-value and low-exposure.
Level two
OSINT
Open-source intelligence: everything public, found by a trained analyst and read in the original language.
All of level one, plus
Filings and court records at source
Ownership and structure mapping
Litigation and insolvency history
Open media and social, original language
Deep-web and archive retrieval
Buy this when
You need to know who you are dealing with and the record is reasonably reliable where they operate.
Level three
HUMINT
Human-source intelligence: what is not written down, from people in the jurisdiction who know the subject first-hand.
All of level two, plus
Discreet enquiry through vetted sources
Subject-matter and sector advisors
Reputation with regulators and peers
Undeclared relationships and liabilities
Source graded A–F, information 1–6
Buy this when
The public record is thin, the stake is material, or a person's judgement is the thing you are actually backing.
Level four
Verified intelligence
Level three, with every document and image tested before it reaches the report.
All of level three, plus
Provenance analysis on every exhibit
Generative-media and edit detection
Location claims put to satellite radar
Sealed chain of custody on arrival
Findings prepared to be relied upon
Buy this when
The finding may be challenged — by a board, a regulator, a court, or the other side.
Each level includes the one before it. We will recommend the lowest level that answers your question, and say plainly when a higher one would not change the answer.
At every level
Conflict-checked first
Cleared before we accept. We do not act on both sides of a matter, and we tell you when we must decline.
Lawful throughout
No pretexting against regulated data, no unlawful interception, no material obtained improperly. If it would not survive disclosure, we do not gather it.
Your identity protected
Sources never learn who is asking. Instructions can be taken through counsel where privilege matters.
Fixed scope, agreed cost
Question, timetable and fee in writing before work begins. Extensions are proposed, never assumed.
One partner, start to finish
The person who takes the instruction runs the enquiry and writes the report.
Graded, not asserted
What we could not establish is stated as plainly as what we could.
Beyond a single mandate
Standing programmes for those who decide often.
Portfolio monitoring
Counterparties, partners and principals re-screened on a cycle you set, with human-source refresh where the record is thin.
Market watch
Political, regulatory and security developments in a jurisdiction, read by someone who is there, delivered when they matter rather than on a schedule.
Retained counsel support
A standing arrangement with a firm's disputes or transactions team: conflict-checked, ready to instruct, evidence handled to the standard the matter will need.
Not sure which level a question needs? Describe the decision it has to support and we will tell you — including when the honest answer is that level one will do.
