Sectors

We do not claim every sector.

Metals and mining · Oil and gas · Renewable energy — wind, solar, hydro, hydrogen, biofuels · Carbon capture and storage · Agriculture and soft commodities
Our deepest bench, and where our in-country network is concentrated.

Banking and capital markets · Private equity and venture · Hedge funds · Wealth management · Pension funds and institutional investors · Insurance
